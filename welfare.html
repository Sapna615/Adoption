ANIMAL WELFARE

When an animal needs help, start with safety.

Keep distance, avoid risky handling and contact an appropriate veterinary or animal-welfare service for urgent situations.

01

Found an animal

Observe from a safe distance. Note location, condition and identifying features.

02

Injured animal

Do not attempt treatment unless qualified. Contact a veterinarian or appropriate rescue service.

03

Possible aggression

Do not corner or chase the animal. Keep people and children away and seek trained help.

04

Share useful details

Provide the location, time, photos if safe, and a concise description to the people assisting.

Important: PetHaven is an educational portfolio demo. For an urgent animal or human safety emergency, contact the appropriate local emergency, veterinary or animal-welfare service.
